Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TalksyLingo ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the TalksyLingo Android application. TalksyLingo is an 18+ audio language-practice and conversation app.

1. Information We Collect and Process

TalksyLingo does not require your real name, phone number, or email address to use the app. To operate the service, we process the following limited data:

  • Anonymous Account & Device Identifiers: An anonymous Firebase Authentication UID, a hashed device identifier (derived from Android ID), and a randomly generated Talksy ID (e.g., TLK-XXXXXXXX) used to maintain your session, call level progress, and daily free coin balance.
  • Onboarding & Matchmaking Preferences: Your self-selected gender, 18+ age confirmation, selected region/room, and language preferences used to match you with a conversation partner.
  • Call Signaling Data (Temporary): Temporary WebRTC session descriptions (SDP offers/answers) and ICE network candidates exchanged via Firebase Realtime Database to establish peer-to-peer voice calls. Signaling rooms are automatically deleted when a call ends.
  • Safety, Blocking & Rating Records: Post-call star ratings, call duration metadata, user block records, and abuse reports submitted during or after a call to enforce our Community Guidelines.

2. Microphone Permission & Live Audio

TalksyLingo requires microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) to transmit your voice during active calls. We do not record, store, or transcribe your voice conversations. All voice audio is streamed live between participants using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. You can revoke microphone access at any time in your Android device settings.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to:

  • Authenticate your anonymous session and save your speaking level progress.
  • Match you with other users in your chosen region and language room.
  • Establish and maintain real-time WebRTC audio connections.
  • Enforce user blocks, review abuse reports, and maintain community safety.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

TalksyLingo uses Google Firebase (Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database) to handle authentication, matchmaking queues, and WebRTC call signaling, as well as Google STUN servers to establish peer-to-peer network routes. Data processed by Google Firebase is governed by the Privacy and Security in Firebase terms.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Security

All data transmitted between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and WebRTC voice streams are encrypted via DTLS-SRTP. Temporary matchmaking queue entries and call signaling rooms are automatically removed upon call completion or disconnection. Account profile and block records are retained until you delete your account.

6. Blocking and Reporting Users

During any call, you can tap the Block or Report buttons. Blocking a user stores their identifier locally and in our database so you will not be matched with that user again.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • Inside the App: Open TalksyLingo, tap Legal & Safety on the home screen, and tap "Delete my account & data". This immediately deletes your user profile, matchmaking queue data, block list, local preferences, and anonymous Firebase account.
  • Via the Web (If You Uninstalled the App): Visit our Account Deletion page or email us with your Talksy ID to request manual deletion.

8. Age Restriction (18+ Only) & Child Safety Standards

TalksyLingo is strictly intended for adults aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly allow minors under 18 to use the service. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any user violating these standards will be permanently banned and reported to the appropriate authorities.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app evolves. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
