Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TalksyLingo handles information when you use our voice chat application. Please read it carefully before using the app.

1. Information We May Handle

Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, TalksyLingo may process the following types of information:

  • Account and device information: identifiers used to recognize your app account or device and maintain app functionality.
  • Voice and call information: microphone audio during a voice call, connection information, and temporary signalling data needed to establish and maintain calls.
  • Matchmaking information: information needed to find another user and manage the matchmaking queue.
  • Blocking preferences: information about users you choose to block, depending on where those preferences are stored by the app.
  • Technical information: diagnostic information processed by the app or its service providers, if enabled.

2. Microphone Permission

TalksyLingo requires microphone access for voice conversations. Your device should request this permission before microphone functionality is used. You can manage microphone permission in your Android settings. Denying permission may prevent voice calls from working.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Enable account and app functionality.
  • Match users and establish voice connections.
  • Maintain call signalling and connection status.
  • Apply blocking preferences and support user safety.
  • Diagnose technical problems and protect the service.

4. Firebase and Service Providers

TalksyLingo uses Firebase services for certain app functions, including real-time signalling and matchmaking. Information processed by these services is subject to the applicable service configuration and provider privacy terms.

Voice connections may also use third-party connectivity services to help establish calls. The exact information processed depends on the connection method and services configured in the app.

5. Voice Conversations

Voice calls transmit audio between participants. Do not assume conversations are confidential from the other participant. This policy does not claim that calls are recorded or stored; any recording or retention practices must be described here if the app implements them.

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information. Verify this statement against all advertising, analytics, and other integrations before publishing.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the app's implementation.

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Blocking Other Users

TalksyLingo provides blocking functionality where supported. Blocking preferences may be stored locally or on a server, depending on the implemented feature. Blocking is intended to help prevent unwanted interactions; verify the app's actual behavior before relying on this description.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can find account deletion instructions on our Account Deletion page. Deleting an account should remove the account data covered by the deletion process, subject to legitimate retention requirements. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or records that must be retained by law.

10. Children's Privacy

TalksyLingo is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law or the app's published age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of these requirements, contact us.

11. Your Choices

You can manage microphone permission through Android settings. You may also use the account deletion process described above. Other available choices depend on the features provided in the app and applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
